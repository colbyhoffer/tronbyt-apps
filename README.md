# tronbyt-apps

Personal custom apps for my [Tronbyt](https://github.com/tronbyt/server) server.
Set as the Custom Apps Repository under Settings → Content.

## Apps

### Setlist Tracker (`apps/setlist`)

Shows a concert setlist from [setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm) on the 64x32 display:
artist, venue, date + song count, and a scrolling marquee of the songs.

Modes:

- **My last concert** — most recent show marked attended on your setlist.fm account
- **Random concert I attended** — a random show from your attended history, new pick each render
- **Artist's latest setlist** — newest setlist for any artist
- **Stats** — Top artists seen, Top venues, Top songs heard, or Recent concerts, computed
  from up to 200 attended shows (6 h cache)

Layouts for the concert modes: **Ticket Stub** (date block + perforation, default),
**Tracklist** (header bar + vertically scrolling numbered songs), **Classic** (stacked marquees).
Options: scroll speed, accent color, show songs.

Setup: get a free API key at [setlist.fm/settings/apps](https://www.setlist.fm/settings/apps),
then enter it with your setlist.fm username (or an artist name) in the app settings.

### TCEQ Refinery Alerts (`apps/tceq_alerts`)

Watches the [TCEQ Air Emission Event Report RSS feed](https://www2.tceq.texas.gov/oce/eer/RSSDataFeed.xml)
and shows the newest event whose title contains a keyword (default `REFINERY`): facility name,
county + start time, and incident status + cause (pulled from the event's detail page and cached).
Turn on "Rotate Through All" to step through every match, one per render.

A pixel-art refinery with a flickering flare fills the left 20px; when rotating, the underline
below the header doubles as a position indicator. The sprite is generated by `apps/tceq_alerts/sprite_gen.py`.

Options: keyword, max age (1/2/3 days or the whole ~7-day feed), rotate through all, fetch details on/off,
hide the app when there are no matches, scroll speed, accent color, demo mode.

No API key needed.

### Readwise Quote (`apps/readwise_quote`)

Shows one of your [Readwise](https://readwise.io) highlights: the quote wrapped and scrolling
vertically, followed by the book title and author. Short quotes sit still; long ones roll through.

Sources:

- **Today's Daily Review** — the highlights Readwise picked for today's review
- **Whole library** — every highlight in your account (export endpoint, cached 6 h)

Pick: **same quote all day** (seeded from the date in your device's timezone) or
**next quote each render**.

Layouts: **Logo + scroll** (hand-drawn 16x16 pixel Readwise mark in the left column, default),
**Header + scroll** (7x7 mark + READWISE wordmark over a rule), or **Full screen**. The marks are
drawn from `render.Box` runs so they follow the accent color.
Options: max quote length, scroll speed, accent color. Demo quote when no token is set.

Setup: copy your token from [readwise.io/access_token](https://readwise.io/access_token)
and paste it into the app settings.

## Development


```
pixlet serve apps/setlist/setlist.star
pixlet serve apps/tceq_alerts/tceq_alerts.star
pixlet serve apps/readwise_quote/readwise_quote.star
```

Renders live at http://localhost:8080 with a config form.

### MBA Countdown (`apps/mba_countdown`)

Counts down the calendar days to McCombs MBA graduation (default **May 7, 2027**) on the 64x32 display,
under a pixel Longhorn silhouette and a TEXAS / McCombs lockup in burnt orange.

Layouts:

- **Lockup** (default) — the count in 6x13 with `DAYS` beside it, and a footer that alternates between
  `UNTIL GRADUATION` and the date
- **Big Number** — the count in 10x20 with a `DAYS / MAY 7 / 2027` label column; one still frame, readable
  from across the room

Set a **Program Start** date and the rule under the header fills up as a progress bar. On the day it
switches to `TODAY!` / `HOOK 'EM HORNS`, and afterwards to `MBA DONE` with the class year.

Days are counted in the device's timezone, so the number rolls over at midnight. The Longhorn is
drawn from `render.Box` runs and follows the accent color. Text is trimmed to its glyph rows with
`render.Text(height, offset)` so no rows go to blank descender lines.

Options: graduation date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), layout, event name (long names scroll), days vs. weeks + days,
program start, the two header lines, accent color. No API key needed.
